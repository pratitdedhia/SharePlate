## 1. Key Researched Facts vs Working Assumptions

### Research Notes (Citation Review Required)
- The problem framing references UNEP Food Waste Index 2024, FAO food-loss material, and FSSAI guidance. Verify each statistic and use a direct source citation before presenting quantitative claims in the public submission.
- This repository does not contain field-study records or interview evidence. The user pain points below are design hypotheses, not validated findings.

### Working Assumptions (To Be Tested in Field Deployment)
- Canteen staff are willing to take 90 seconds after lunch/dinner service to snap a photo and list remaining portions if the process is simple and frictionless.
- Recipient NGOs have access to insulated containers or volunteer two-wheelers capable of collecting batches within a 2-hour window.
