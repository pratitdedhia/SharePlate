# 02. Problem Research & Food Waste in India

**Research Pillar:** Tech for Social Good  
**References:** UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024, FAO Global Food Losses, FSSAI Guidelines  

---

## 1. Key Researched Facts vs Working Assumptions

### Researched Facts (Grounded in Verified Data)
- **Urban Waste Volume:** According to the *UNEP Food Waste Index Report (2024)*, Indian households and commercial sectors generate approximately 68.7 million tonnes of food waste annually.
- **Greenhouse Gas Contribution:** Food decaying in anaerobic landfill conditions produces massive quantities of methane (CH₄), which possesses a Global Warming Potential (GWP) 28 times greater than carbon dioxide over a 100-year timescale.
- **Nutritional Deficit:** The Global Hunger Index repeatedly highlights acute micronutrient deficiencies and hunger across urban peri-urban settlements in major Indian metropolitan hubs (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Pune).

### Working Assumptions (To Be Tested in Field Deployment)
- Canteen staff are willing to take 90 seconds after lunch/dinner service to snap a photo and list remaining portions if the process is simple and frictionless.
- Recipient NGOs have access to insulated containers or volunteer two-wheelers capable of collecting batches within a 2-hour window.

## 2. Target User Pain Points

| User Group | Core Pain Point | Current Inefficient Workaround | SharePlate Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **College Canteens & Caterers** | Guilt and disposal costs of throwing away 30–60 meal portions after daily lunch rushes. | Informal WhatsApp group messages that often go unanswered. | Instant listing creation with automated countdown timer and Cloudinary visual proof. |
| **Community NGOs & Shelters** | Unpredictable food availability; uncertainty about food hygiene, allergens, and pickup point. | Cold-calling banquet halls late at night without knowing what is available. | Categorized, searchable feed with clear allergen tags and atomic reservation. |
| **Volunteers & Runners** | Confusion regarding exact loading docks, vehicle ramp security passes, or contact numbers. | Delayed pickups causing food to cool down or miss meal distribution hours. | Protected pickup instructions unlocked only upon confirmed claim. |
