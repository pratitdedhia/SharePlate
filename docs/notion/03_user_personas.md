# 03. Target User Personas

---

## Persona 1: Priya Sharma — Campus Canteen Supervisor
- **Role:** Food Donor
- **Organization:** Central Campus Dining Hall, DTU (Delhi Technological University)
- **Context:** Operates a high-volume student dining hall serving 1,200 students daily. Despite careful planning, weather variations and exam schedules leave 30–50 portions of freshly cooked biryani or dal-rice unsold after lunch service.
- **Goals:** Quickly list surplus food before it sits for hours; ensure food goes to genuine non-profits; keep dining hall loading ramp clean.
- **Frustrations:** Dislikes complex inventory software. Cannot answer phone calls while supervising kitchen cleanup.
- **Key Feature Needed:** One-page mobile-friendly donation form with quick photo upload and precise gate instructions.

---

## Persona 2: Rajesh Kumar — Non-Profit Operations Coordinator
- **Role:** Food Recipient
- **Organization:** Robin Hood Army, North Delhi Cluster
- **Context:** Coordinates distribution to three community shelters and evening learning centers in Rohini. Needs reliable nutritious food batches between 3:00 PM and 7:00 PM.
- **Goals:** Discover wholesome, hot meals nearby; claim exact portion quantities matching shelter capacity; ensure volunteer safety.
- **Frustrations:** Wasting fuel and time driving to canteens only to find another NGO already collected the food.
- **Key Feature Needed:** Atomic reservation button that locks portion availability instantly, combined with verified pickup address and allergen labels.

---

## Persona 3: Ananya Sen — Logistics Volunteer Runner
- **Role:** Volunteer Logistics
- **Organization:** Youth Food Relief
- **Context:** College student with a two-wheeler who helps pick up food containers and deliver them to transit camp distribution points.
- **Goals:** View clear pickup and delivery instructions; confirm arrival and handoff in two taps.
- **Frustrations:** Long wait times at security gates because donor phone numbers were not provided.
- **Key Feature Needed:** Clear lifecycle status toggle (`Mark Picked Up` → `Confirm Delivery`).
