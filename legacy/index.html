Factual Food Safety & Mutual Responsibility

SharePlate facilitates communication between food donors and community organizations. SharePlate does not certify food safety. Donors must provide accurate ingredients, freshness timestamps, and allergen details. Recipients must inspect food condition upon pickup.

About SharePlate

Built for Hacktober HackDays 2026 · SheBuilds Delhi Chapter · Powered by MLH

The Problem

Over 68 million tonnes of food are wasted annually in India, while commercial kitchens struggle to find prompt local NGOs to pick up daily surplus batches. SharePlate bridges this gap through fast, concurrency-safe reservations and Cloudinary visual verification.

Cloudinary Mandatory Integration

Why Cloudinary is Essential to SharePlate

  • • Visual Inspection: Shelters inspect food packaging and quality before committing volunteer runners.
  • • Responsive Delivery: Automatic dynamic compression (f_auto, q_auto) shrinks camera uploads from 4MB to ~320KB, saving volunteer mobile bandwidth.
  • • Server-Signed Uploads: API secrets stay protected on backend while browser uploads directly to Cloudinary.

Food Safety Principles

SharePlate is a communication platform. We do not inspect or certify food safety. Donors must provide truthful ingredient, allergen, and freshness information. Shelters must verify temperatures upon pickup.
